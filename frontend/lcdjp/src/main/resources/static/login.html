Who can log in

Access depends on your role. Most citizens register themselves; a few roles are issued directly by an administrator for verification reasons.

Self-registered

Citizens

Create a free account with your name, phone number and national ID to start reporting cases.

Self-registered

Lawyers

Register with your work ID and certificate for verification, then get matched to cases.

Admin-issued

Legal organisations

These accounts are created by a System Administrator. Contact your JusticeLink admin to get one set up.

Trouble logging in?

Forgot your password

Use the "Forgot password" link on the login page with your registered email address to reset it.
