Help build access to justice in Uganda

JusticeLink is a small team working on the software, verification and administration behind a national access-to-justice platform.

The kind of work involved

Based on what the platform does day to day, the areas most likely to come up are:

Software engineering

Building and maintaining the citizen, legal-professional and administrator dashboards.

Verification & case operations

Reviewing lawyer credentials and keeping cases moving between citizens, legal aid and government offices.

Community & language access

Keeping the platform genuinely usable across the languages and regions it serves.

JusticeLink Uganda

A digital platform connecting citizens to Local Council offices, police services and legal aid.
