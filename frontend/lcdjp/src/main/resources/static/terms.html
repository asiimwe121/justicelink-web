1. About JusticeLink

JusticeLink is intended to improve access to justice by helping users:

  • identify appropriate channels for reporting or resolving matters;
  • submit and track justice-related cases or requests;
  • access legal information;
  • locate nearby justice, police, legal-aid and other relevant services;
  • communicate information to authorized institutions;
  • receive notifications relating to submitted matters; and
  • obtain AI-assisted legal information and case-routing assistance.

JusticeLink does not replace courts, police authorities, Local Council institutions, lawyers, legal-aid organizations, government authorities, or other legally authorized institutions.
