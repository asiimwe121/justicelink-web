The fastest way to get help depends on what you need: an account issue, or urgent legal help right now.

Account & platform support

Already have a JusticeLink account? Sign in and use the Contact Support chat inside the app. It's the quickest way to reach us about a case, a login issue, or anything account-related.

General enquiries

For anything else, email us at support@justicelinkug.org or info@justicelinkug.org, or call/WhatsApp +256 743 334 882.

Reporting an emergency? JusticeLink is a case-management platform, not an emergency line. For an emergency in progress, contact the Uganda Police Force or your nearest police station directly.
