Our Mission

Closing the distance between citizens and justice

JusticeLink Uganda is a digital platform that connects citizens directly to Local Council offices, police services and legal aid, in the language they're most comfortable using.

Create a free account See what the platform does

Why JusticeLink exists

Getting help with a Local Council matter, a police report, or legal aid in Uganda often means knowing exactly which office to visit and being able to get there. JusticeLink puts that access on a phone instead. A citizen can report an issue, get matched with the right service, and follow its progress without a physical trip to start the process.

  • Citizens can report an incident or request, find the nearest Local Council office, police station or legal aid clinic on a map, and track what happens to their case.
  • Lawyers and legal organisations can be matched to citizens who need representation and communicate with them securely inside the platform.
  • Police officers, District Administrators and System Administrators get role-based dashboards for verification, oversight and reporting. These accounts are issued directly by an administrator rather than self-registered.

How it works

Every case moves through the same three stages, whether it starts as an incident report or a request for legal help.

Report

A citizen submits a case (an incident, a request, or a need for legal help) from the citizen dashboard.

Match & assign

The case is matched to the right lawyer, Local Council office or police contact and routed to them for action.

Resolve & track

Updates, secure messages and notifications keep the citizen informed until the case is closed.

Built for how Uganda actually communicates
